Privacy Policy for SplitX

Last updated: October 22, 2025

SplitX ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Account Information: Username, email address, and password (encrypted)
  • Payment Information: UPI ID for facilitating payment settlements
  • Profile Information: Display name and profile preferences

Usage Data

  • Expense Data: Expenses you create, split, and track
  • Group Information: Groups you create or join, and group members
  • Transaction History: Payment settlements and confirmations
  • Messages: Chat messages within groups and with friends

Device Information

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Device identifiers for push notifications

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our expense splitting service
  • To facilitate payment settlements between users
  • To send push notifications about expenses, payments, and group activities
  • To improve app functionality and user experience
  • To provide customer support
  • To detect and prevent fraud or abuse

3. Data Storage and Security

We use Firebase services (Google Cloud Platform) to store and process your data:

  • Firebase Authentication: Securely manages user accounts
  • Cloud Firestore: Stores expense data, groups, and messages
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Delivers push notifications

All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and at rest using Firebase's encryption standards. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With Other Users: Your username and expense information is visible to group members you choose to share with
  • Service Providers: Firebase/Google Cloud for hosting and infrastructure
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights

5. Payment Information

SplitX does not process payments directly. We store your UPI ID to facilitate payment coordination, but all actual payment transactions are handled by your UPI payment provider (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.). We are not responsible for the security of transactions processed by third-party payment providers.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct your information in the app
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt-out: Disable push notifications in your device settings
  • Data Portability: Request export of your data

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

SplitX is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the app and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and maintained on servers located outside of your country. By using SplitX, you consent to the transfer of information to countries outside your country of residence, which may have different data protection rules.

11. Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, and notifications - Firebase Privacy Policy
  • UPI Payment Providers: For payment processing (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.)
